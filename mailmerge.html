MailMerge AI

MailMerge AI is a Google Sheets add-on that helps users draft customized cold outreach emails and personalized content directly inside spreadsheets using AI automation.

App Purpose & Features

The add-on analyzes selected spreadsheet row data and automatically generates tailored email subject lines and personalized body drafts into dedicated columns, eliminating manual copywriting friction.

Data Access & Privacy Policy

  • Minimal Sheet Scope: Operates strictly on the active spreadsheet via spreadsheets.currentonly. It does not access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • External Requests: Communicates directly with AI endpoints via script.external_request strictly upon explicit user generation action.
  • No Data Retention: User sheet data is not logged, stored, or shared with third parties.

Terms of Service

MailMerge AI is provided 'as is' for workflow productivity. Users are responsible for reviewing AI-generated drafts prior to any external outreach.
