AdSpend AI

Automated ROAS & Inventory Budget Auditor for E-commerce Media Buyers

App Overview & Key Features

AdSpend AI is a specialized Google Workspace add-on built to assist performance marketers, media buyers, and e-commerce entrepreneurs in auditing marketing ad performance instantly inside Google Sheets.

Core Functionalities:

  • Automatic ROAS Calculation: Instantly calculates Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) across all campaign rows.
  • Inventory & Burn-Rate Correlation: Alerts media buyers when high-performing ad sets risk burning out due to low stock inventory.
  • AI Actionable Recommendations: Generates concrete next steps (e.g., "🔥 SCALE BUDGET +30%", "⛔ PAUSE IMMEDIATELY", or "⚠️ LOW INVENTORY ALERT") in a formatted output column.

Data Usage & Permission Scopes

AdSpend AI requests strictly the minimum required permissions to function:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly: Used strictly to read campaign metrics in your currently active sheet and append the calculated ROAS and AI recommendation columns back into your active sheet upon user initiation. It cannot access any other spreadsheets in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request: Required to send selected row metrics to the AI language processing server (OpenAI API) via encrypted HTTPS calls to return tactical marketing insights.
