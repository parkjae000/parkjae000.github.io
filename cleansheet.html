CleanSheet AI

Application Purpose: CleanSheet AI is a professional data cleansing add-on designed for Google Sheets users. It automates tedious data sanitation tasks, helping businesses eliminate errors and maintain clean datasets effortlessly.

Core Application Features

  • Smart Deduplication: Instantly scans, detects, and removes duplicate rows based on selected key columns without corrupting original data structures.
  • Whitespace & Text Sanitation: Trims accidental leading, trailing, and excessive spaces, while standardizing uppercase/lowercase text formatting.
  • Data Validation & Format Standardizing: Automatically detects broken email patterns, normalizes date formats (YYYY-MM-DD), and removes empty ghost rows.

Permission Scopes Usage

CleanSheet AI only requests access to spreadsheets.currentonly strictly to inspect and format data within the active sheet selected by the user. We never read or modify external spreadsheets.
