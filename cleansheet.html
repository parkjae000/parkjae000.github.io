CleanSheet AI

Smart Data Cleansing, Deduplication & Formatting Tool

Application Purpose & Overview

CleanSheet AI is a professional productivity add-on for Google Sheets™ designed to automate dataset sanitation. It helps businesses, marketers, and analysts eliminate duplicate rows, strip unwanted whitespace, and standardize messy cell formats in seconds.

Core Functionalities:

  • Smart Deduplication: Identifies and safely removes duplicate rows according to user-selected key columns.
  • Whitespace & Text Sanitation: Automatically trims leading, trailing, and duplicate spaces, and normalizes uppercase/lowercase casing.
  • Data Formatting: Normalizes date formats (YYYY-MM-DD), fixes phone/email casing, and clears empty ghost rows.

Privacy Policy & Google User Data Disclosures

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

CleanSheet AI ("we", "our", or "the add-on") is committed to protecting user privacy. This section explicitly details our data practices regarding Google user data in compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Google User Data Accessed

CleanSheet AI requests strictly the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly scope. Our application accesses only the data (cell values, column headers, and sheet ranges) within the single Google Sheets spreadsheet currently opened and selected by the user. We do not access your Google Drive files, email content, personal contacts, or any spreadsheets other than the active file.

2. How We Use Google User Data

The accessed spreadsheet data is used strictly and solely to execute user-initiated data cleaning tasks in real-time (such as identifying duplicate values, trimming whitespaces, or reformatting dates). Data is processed ephemerally within the client-side Google Apps Script execution runtime to update cell values directly inside the user's active spreadsheet.

3. Data Sharing, Transfer & Third-Party Disclosure

CleanSheet AI does not share, sell, rent, lease, or transfer Google user data to any third parties, advertisers, or external services. All data processing occurs directly within your Google Workspace account environment. No user data is transferred across external networks or exposed to outside vendors.

4. Data Protection & Security Mechanisms

Because CleanSheet AI runs purely within the secure Google Apps Script sandbox environment, your data is protected by Google's enterprise-grade infrastructure. All communications between your browser and Google servers occur over industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. We do not transmit or store spreadsheet contents outside of Google's secure ecosystem.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

CleanSheet AI practices a zero-retention policy. We do not maintain any external databases or server logs storing your spreadsheet contents. All data operations are ephemeral and cease immediately once the cleaning operation is complete. Users can instantly revoke add-on permissions at any time via their Google Account Security Settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
